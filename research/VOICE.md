# Ramanujan voice guide

Draft for approval · 2026-11-03 · Based on `research/content-audit.md`

## The idea in one line

**One bold line, plain everything else.** "The Godfathers of Applied AI" is our signature, the way the category leaders each keep one striking line. Every other sentence reads like a senior research partner briefing an executive: precise, calm, checkable.

## Brand anchors (the only places the bold voice lives)

| Anchor | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| "The Godfathers of Applied AI" | Tagline, hero eyebrow, footer, page titles, social cards |
| "AI has a godfather. It's us." | Home hero headline only |

**Definition sentence** (unchanged, repeated verbatim wherever we introduce ourselves): *Ramanujan is an applied AI studio that trains and fine-tunes custom language models, builds niche training datasets, and ships production AI agent systems for businesses.*

The positioning stays as it is on the site today: an applied AI studio for businesses. RL and post-training appear as methods we use, not as the category we sell.

## Tone

**Confident, precise, measured, plain, accountable.**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| State what we do and how it is checked | Promise outcomes with adjectives |
| Explain the mechanism (filtered, labelled, evaluated on held-out cases) | Use slang or bravado ("junk gets whacked", "zero mercy") |
| Use "we" for the studio, "you" for the reader | Talk about competitors ("everyone else is playing") |
| Say what we won't do ("If you don't need us, we'll say so") | Use scarcity or urgency ("not everyone", "act now") |
| Use numbers only when true and sourced | Use labour-replacement jokes ("never asks for a raise", "no sick days") |
| Keep one idea per sentence, mostly under 20 words | Imply clients, results or case studies we don't have |
| Use full stops and commas | Use exclamation marks, emojis or em dashes |

## Vocabulary

**Use:** fine-tune, supervised fine-tuning, preference training, evaluation, held-out test cases, release gates, acceptance criteria, training dataset, personal data removed, versioned, reproducible, deploy, monitor, retrain, pilot, scoping, you own the model, your infrastructure, model-agnostic.

**Use sparingly (light RL wording):** post-training, reinforcement learning, reward signals, rubrics, preference data. Use these in method descriptions (Train & tune, Datasets, the FAQ), never as a headline category.

**Retire:** unleash, bend it to our will, armies, crush, dominate, nervous competitors, prints money, zero mercy, no days off, never sleeps, genius in a jar, untouchable*, sit-down*, the family business*, pay up*, often imitated, refuse to be average, (You need us.)

## Honesty checklist (run on every page)

1. No client names, logos, testimonials, results or case studies until real ones exist.
2. No workforce, volume or market-share numbers.
3. Demos and sample data are labelled "Illustrative example".
4. No certification is claimed until it has been audited.
5. Approved promises only: a plan, a price and an honest verdict within 24 hours; a focused pilot in two to six weeks; work inside your own cloud with NDAs and DPAs; you own the model weights; if you don't need us, we'll say so.
